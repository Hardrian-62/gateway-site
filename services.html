What We Offer

Our Services

Strategy, local insight and practical support for expanding into Latin America.

Our core is international expansion consulting. We help European companies understand where the opportunities are, how the market works, and what it takes to establish themselves successfully.

Alongside strategy we bring together digital positioning, marketing, localisation and cross-cultural communication, because entering a new market is not just about having the right strategy. It is about making that strategy work in the real world.

Based in Florianópolis and connected across Europe and Latin America, we work from within the market, not just from a distance.

01
Direction

Consulting — International Expansion Strategy

Expand into Latin America with clarity and confidence.

Entering a new market is a major decision. There are opportunities to find, but also regulations, business cultures, partners and practical realities that can be difficult to understand from Europe.

We help you make sense of the market before you commit, and turn your plans into a realistic route forward.

Our experience comes from having worked on the ground, built businesses and operations in Latin America, developed local partnerships and helped companies navigate the realities of doing business here.

We don't just provide a strategy. We help you understand what it means in practice.

Our consulting services include

International Expansion Consulting

Strategic guidance to help you assess opportunities, understand the market and determine whether, and how, expansion into Latin America makes sense for your business.

Market Entry & Go-to-Market Strategy

Develop a practical market-entry approach based on your business objectives, target market, product or service, and the realities of the region.

Business Development & Partnership Support

Identify potential partners, distributors and other local stakeholders, and support you in building relationships that can create lasting business opportunities.

Operational Strategy & Market Structure

Understand the structures, processes and local considerations you need to establish a sustainable presence and operate effectively in the market.

Market Intelligence & Risk Assessment

Gain a clearer picture of your market, competitors, opportunities and potential risks, so you can make informed decisions before investing time and resources.

What this means for you

  • Make informed decisions before committing to a new market
  • Avoid costly misunderstandings and unnecessary detours
  • Understand local business realities, not just market statistics
  • Find the right partners and opportunities more efficiently
  • Turn your expansion plans into practical next steps
02
Visibility

Digital Positioning & Marketing

Be visible, relevant and understood in your new market.

Entering a market is one thing. Being found, and trusted, is another.

Your existing website, brand and marketing may work perfectly well in Europe and still fail to connect with customers in Latin America. Search behaviour, expectations, language and communication styles can all be different.

We help you adapt your digital presence and marketing to the market you are entering, while keeping your brand and business objectives firmly at the centre.

And because our marketing expertise is connected to our market-entry work, we don't approach Latin America as an abstract audience. We understand the context behind the market.

Our marketing & positioning services include

Digital Market Readiness & Positioning

Prepare your brand, messaging and digital presence for the Latin American market, making sure your positioning is relevant, credible and locally appropriate.

SEO/GEO & Content Strategy

Improve your visibility with multilingual SEO, GEO and content strategies designed around how people in your target markets actually search for information.

Performance Marketing Strategy

Develop targeted digital marketing strategies for lead generation, customer acquisition and growth, adapted to the realities of your chosen Latin American market.

Brand Localisation

Adapt your brand messaging and communication to local cultural and market contexts without losing the identity that makes your business distinctive.

Multilingual Communication

Develop clear and consistent communication across English, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, so your brand can speak naturally to different audiences.

What this means for you

  • Be easier to find in your target market
  • Communicate in a way that feels natural and relevant locally
  • Build credibility with customers and business partners
  • Keep your international brand consistent while adapting it to local expectations
  • Turn your digital presence into a genuine part of your market-entry strategy

03
Connection

Localisation & Cross-Cultural Communication

Make your message work across cultures, not just languages.

Tone, formality, humour, context and even what is left unsaid can change the way a message is understood. What feels perfectly natural in Germany or the UK may sound distant, confusing or simply wrong in Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, or elsewhere in Latin America.

We help bridge that gap.

From translation and localisation to brand communication and cross-cultural support, we make sure your message remains clear, credible and culturally relevant wherever it is being heard.

Our team brings experience from both sides of the Atlantic and works across German, English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.

Our cross-cultural communication services include

Translation & Localisation

Professional translation and localisation of websites, marketing materials, business documents and digital content between English, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, adapted to the people and market you are actually speaking to.

Multilingual Brand Communication

Adapt your brand messaging and marketing communication so that it feels natural and consistent across different markets, while retaining your core brand identity.

Cross-Cultural Communication Support

Navigate differences in communication styles, business practices and expectations to build stronger relationships with customers, partners and stakeholders.

Quality Assurance

Review translated and localised content for linguistic accuracy, cultural appropriateness, consistency and alignment with your brand.

Audio & Video Advisory

Support for multilingual audio and video projects, including voice-over, subtitles, localisation and content adaptation for European and Latin American audiences.

What this means for you

  • Communicate naturally and confidently across languages and cultures
  • Reduce misunderstandings with customers, partners and stakeholders
  • Protect the integrity of your brand across markets
  • Build trust by communicating in a way that feels local
  • Make your international presence feel genuinely connected, not simply translated
